Threat Assessment Methodology & Classification Standards

Assessments are defensibly derived from verified impact on confidentiality, integrity, and availability:

CRITICAL THREAT

Confirmed ransomware encryption or multi-million record citizen PII exfiltration affecting primary national operations, core banking, or intelligence registries.

HIGH THREAT

Significant internal database compromise, verified administrative credentials leaked, or extortion listings with active countdowns on designated KPI entities.

MEDIUM THREAT

Targeted exposure of commercial supply-chain partners, secondary web portals, or uncorroborated darknet forum database sales.

LOW THREAT

Defacements, legacy forum claims with low credibility, or minor unauthenticated metadata disclosures.

NOT ASSESSED

No public claim located in the collected research as of 2026-10-26. Explicitly does not imply verified security posture.

2. Scope & Legal Disclaimers

This dossier operates under strict passive observation guidelines. No automated darknet web crawling, active vulnerability scanning, authentication bypassing, or live threat-actor engagement was performed. The absence of a public claim for any particular institution does not certify that entity as secure; it is recorded explicitly as "No public claim located (as of 2026-10-26)". All citizen PII, credentials, and sensitive authentications have been redacted prior to report compilation.
